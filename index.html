Game Rules

Town Roles

Sheriff: Investigates players, reveals alignment (Good/Bad/Neutral)

Investigator: Investigates players, reveals exact role

Doctor: Heals one player per night, prevents night kills

Bodyguard: Protects a player; if attacked, both die

Vigilante: Shoots at night; survives if kills Mafia, dies otherwise

Veteran: Goes on alert (3 uses); kills all visitors while alert

Trapper: Places traps (3 total); kills first visitor

Mafia Roles

Godfather: Commands Mafia kill, appears good to Sheriff

Mafioso: Performs Mafia kill

Framer: Makes target appear suspicious to Sheriff

Janitor: Cleans one Mafia kill, hides role

Neutral Roles

Jester: Wins if lynched

Executioner: Wins if target is lynched

Survivor: Wins with any faction if alive at end

Serial Killer: Kills nightly, immune to night attacks

Arsonist: Douses players, then ignites

Plague Doctor: Infects players, wins when all are infected

Werewolf: Kills every 3rd night, immune on active nights
